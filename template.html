About the numbers & flight records

Each takeoff-to-landing leg counts once. Canceled bookings, duplicates and future flights are excluded. December 2025 is excluded from the default 2026 view. Email records indicate booked travel, check-in or post-flight evidence; they are not all airline-certified flown records. Chiang Mai–Bangkok and the four New Zealand/Australia legs were confirmed by you.

Distance is airport-to-airport great-circle distance, not actual track miles. Airtime is a rough model: distance ÷ 500 mph + 0.35 hours per leg, excluding taxiing and layovers. Winds, routing and aircraft speed can substantially change actual time. Earth laps use a 24,901-mile equatorial circumference. Regions count airport countries/territories separately, including Hong Kong and Taiwan; transits count.

Auckland is inferred as the New Zealand connection. The Auckland–Sydney local date is estimated as July 6 from arrival evidence; the trip began July 4. The Bangkok airport on the Chiang Mai leg is unverified; BKK is used for the map and distance. Unverified flight numbers remain blank. Airline counts are omitted where the carrier is unknown.

Airport coordinates: OurAirports / DataHub. Geography: World Atlas / Natural Earth.
